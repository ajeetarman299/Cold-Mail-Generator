Machine Learning Engineer, Recommendations

Digital Product · Portland, Oregon (hybrid) · Full time

About the role

Our app and website suggest gear for every trip. You will build and run the recommendation models behind those suggestions, from training to serving, and work with product managers to test what helps customers find the right gear.

What you will do

  • Train and improve ranking and recommendation models in Python and TensorFlow.
  • Package models with Docker and deploy them on our Kubernetes platform.
  • Build feature pipelines on our SQL data warehouse.
  • Plan A/B testing with product and analytics partners.

What you bring

  • 3+ years building and shipping machine learning models in production.
  • Strong Python and SQL.
  • Experience with TensorFlow or PyTorch.
  • Experience with Docker and Kubernetes.

What you bring
